## 1. Purpose and scope

This register records the risks to {{code}}: what could make the project fail, how likely and severe each risk is, and what is being done about it. It is reviewed at every gate.

## 2. Rating scales

Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are rated from 1 to 5, and the score is L × I. A score of 15 or more is high, 8 to 14 is medium, and below 8 is low.
